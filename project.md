A Digital Heritage Portal for India's Armed Forces

Presentation Date: 15 August 2026
1. Project Overview
Bharat Rakshak is a digital military heritage portal created using Amazon Kiro. It brings information about India's wars, operations, martyrs, heroes, gallantry awards and military heritage into one structured, searchable and accessible digital experience.
The project follows an offline-first approach so that the core reference experience does not depend on external APIs or continuous internet connectivity.
2. Problem Statement
Military history and service records are valuable, but information is often scattered across books, websites and archives.
Key problems:
Information is difficult to access in one focused place.
Finding complete information about a hero, martyr or award recipient can take time.
Students and citizens need a simple way to explore military heritage.
Connectivity should not be a barrier to core reference information.
3. Proposed Solution
Bharat Rakshak organizes military heritage into a single digital portal with:
Wars & Operations
Martyrs & Heroes
Gallantry Awards
Verified Quotes
Regimental Heritage
Multilingual interface
The solution follows four principles:
Collect → Verify → Discover → Access
4. Objectives
Digitally preserve Indian military heritage.
Make wars, heroes, martyrs and awards easier to explore.
Provide searchable and structured information.
Support multilingual access.
Provide offline-first access to core reference content.
Create an educational resource for students, citizens and researchers.
Provide a scalable foundation for future military-heritage features.
5. Main Features
Wars & Battles
Chronology, operations, theatres and outcomes.
Martyr Profiles
Structured service and historical information with source/verification awareness.
Gallantry Awards
Recipients, award categories and citations.
Quote Explorer
Attributed quotes with context and random discovery.
Multilingual
English plus nine Indian languages in the prototype interface.
Offline & Private
Local-first storage with no unnecessary tracking.
6. Technologies Used

JavaScript — application logic and interactions.
JSON / Records — structured local reference data.
LocalStorage — client-side local storage.

Human review remains important for historical facts, citations and sensitive military information.
